The holidays are a good time to slow down. Here are a few small tips to help FocusGuard fit your days, on your terms.

**Pick your allowed hours.** Blocked sites and apps only open in your allowed hours. They're set to 14:00–19:00 at first, and you can change them in 30-minute steps. Making them shorter is free. Making them wider or later needs a code from your partner. Over the holidays, you might like a little time in the afternoon and calm, quiet evenings with family.

**Add your own sites and apps.** Facebook and X are on the block list to start. If something else pulls you away, add it. It will share the same daily limit. Websites you add are blocked in Chrome. To block another browser, add it as an app.

**A tip for Samsung phones.** Samsung phones sometimes put apps to sleep to save battery. Go to Settings › Battery › Background usage limits › Never sleeping apps, and add FocusGuard, so it keeps working through the day.

**Keep it up to date.** Updates come through Google Play, just like your other apps. They bring fixes and small improvements along the way.
