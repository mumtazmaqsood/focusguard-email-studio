# FocusGuard brand voice

FocusGuard is an Android app that helps people spend less time on Facebook, X and other distracting sites and apps. It gives them one shared daily limit and set allowed hours. Only a partner can unlock more time, with a one-time code. We write for two groups equally:
- **Parents** who set FocusGuard up on their child's phone and act as the partner.
- **Adults** who want to limit themselves, with a spouse or friend as their partner.

## 1. Our voice in three words

**Calm. Kind. Supportive.** We sound like a friendly coach: on the reader's side, encouraging, never judging. We celebrate small steps and talk about what people gain (time, focus, a clear mind), not what they're doing wrong.

- Speak to the reader as "you". Short sentences, everyday words.
- Be warm but clear. Say exactly what to do next.
- Never preachy, scary, guilt-tripping or pushy-salesy.

## 2. Words we use

- focus, stay focused, a clear mind
- time back, more time for reading, work, study, family
- daily limit, allowed hours, block list
- your partner, partner code, set up together
- small steps, one day at a time, a good habit
- calm, balance, your choice, on your terms
- test version, Android, Chrome (when describing what it works with)

## 3. Words we avoid

- addiction, addicted, hooked, "glued to your phone"
- spying, monitoring, tracking, watching (we don't track anything, and it sounds like surveillance)
- control, punish, punishment, ban, lock them out
- lazy, wasting your life, bad habit, guilty, shame
- detox, digital detox (a health claim)
- urgent hype: "last chance", "act now", "don't miss out"
- "100%", "guaranteed", "forever", "never again"

## 4. Claims we must never make

- **No guarantees.** We may say FocusGuard *helps* you focus and gives you time back. We never say it "guarantees" focus, or promise results in a set time (like "focused in 7 days").
- **No "impossible to bypass".** The blocker can still be switched off in Accessibility settings, or the app uninstalled. Don't call it unbreakable or tamper-proof.
- **Not every browser.** Websites are only checked in **Chrome** so far. Don't say it blocks Samsung Internet, Firefox or "all browsers".
- **Android only.** Don't say it works on iPhone. Don't say it's on the Play Store until it actually is. For now, people install it from the APK on GitHub.
- **It's a test version.** Don't call it finished or "final". Invite feedback instead.
- **No health or medical promises.** Don't say it cures phone addiction, ADHD, anxiety or sleep problems, or that it "detoxes" anything.
- **Privacy, only what's true.** We *can* say: no internet permission, no ads, no analytics, nothing leaves your phone. Don't add anything beyond that.
- **No made-up numbers**, such as user counts, reviews or percentages we haven't measured.
